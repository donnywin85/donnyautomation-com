Privacy Policy

For paper-risk-monitor. Dated 13 September 2026.

What the tool reads

paper-risk-monitor reads account details, positions and open orders from the owner's own Alpaca paper trading account. It reads them through Alpaca's OAuth API.

Where the data is stored

That data is stored only on the owner's own machine.

Who the users are

The tool has no users other than the owner of this site. It collects no data from anyone else.

Selling and sharing

The tool sells nothing and shares nothing with anyone.

Cookies and analytics

The tool sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Ending access

Revoking the app in the Alpaca dashboard ends all of its access to the account.

Contact

Send questions through the site's existing contact route: GitHub, at github.com/donnywin85.
