Barcove is billed by Shopify, and the Apify actors are billed by Apify. Those platforms handle your payment details under their own policies.

Barcove: SKU & Barcode Search

A Shopify app that adds a product search box to a store.

What it reads

Through Shopify's Admin API, with the read_products permission only: product titles, descriptions, tags, vendor, product type, variant SKUs and barcodes, and the variant metafields a merchant names as extra barcodes. It does not access customer accounts, orders, addresses or payment details.

What it stores

For each store: a search index of those product fields, the store's myshopify domain, the access token Shopify issues to the app with its refresh token and expiry, the merchant's synonym groups, a count of storefront searches per billing period and the plan name. It does not store the words shoppers type into the search box.

How it is removed

When a merchant uninstalls the app, or Shopify sends a shop data erasure request, the store's index, synonym groups, counts and token are deleted. The app is hosted on Railway in the United States.

StatementLocal

A bank statement converter that runs in your browser.

  • Your PDF is read inside the browser tab and is never uploaded. We never see your file, your transactions or your bank login.
  • The page keeps a few records in your browser's own storage: today's download count with a short fingerprint of each statement, a random install id, and your licence key if you bought Pro.
  • If you use Pro, the page sends the licence key, the install id and the product name to our licence service when you activate and about once a week. The licence service asks Stripe whether the purchase is valid, keeps that answer in memory for up to 10 minutes, has no database and logs only the request method, path, status and a one-way hash of the key.
  • A buy link first passes a short redirect that reads the country our host, Vercel, derives from your IP address, keeps nothing, and sends US visitors on to Stripe.
  • No analytics and no tracking scripts. The site is hosted on Vercel and the licence service on Railway, which may keep standard network logs, including IP addresses.

Self-Publishing Calculator

Free KDP calculators on the web and a Chrome extension with an optional Pro licence.

  • Website: the calculators run in your browser and what you type is not sent anywhere. The site sets no cookies. It uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying visitors. Vercel keeps standard server logs.
  • Extension: saved books, prices and scenarios stay in the extension's storage on your device. The extension never reads, stores or fetches any Amazon or KDP page, and has no analytics.
  • Pro: the extension sends your licence key (the id of your Stripe checkout) and a random install id to our licence service when you activate and about once a week. The checkout page receives the install id so Stripe can pass it back with the payment. The licence service keeps no database, holds Stripe's answer in memory for up to 10 minutes and logs only the request method, path, status and a one-way hash of the key. It is hosted on Railway.

New Adviser Monitor and Audit Prospect List

Lists built from public government records: SEC investment adviser files, and the Department of Labor's Form 5500 datasets.

  • The lists hold firm-level and plan-level fields only. New Adviser Monitor has no compliance officer, owner, employee, phone or email fields; some firms are registered under a person's name and are shown as registered. Audit Prospect List has no signer, administrator, preparer, phone, email or employer identification number.
  • The sites store nothing about visitors, set no cookies and run no analytics.
  • When sales open, Stripe takes payment and keeps the payment record, including your email and billing address. The list is delivered as a private download link.
  • Both sites are hosted on Railway, which may keep standard network logs.

Data actors on Apify

Our actors, including UK Public Tenders and Form D Raise Watch, run on Apify's platform. Apify holds your account, your run inputs and results, and your billing, under the Apify privacy policy.

  • UK Public Tenders returns organisation fields from the official Find a Tender service, never contact names, emails or phone numbers.
  • Form D Raise Watch returns Form D filings as the issuer filed them with the SEC, which can include the names of the related persons the filing lists. These are public records.

If a record about you appears in an actor's output and you want to ask about it, write to us. We will tell you where the public source is, since we do not control the source itself.

Pay-per-call endpoints

The endpoints at x402.donnyautomation.com are paid per call with USDC on the Base network.

  • Each request carries the parameters you send, such as a company ticker, a web address to convert or a name to screen. They are used to answer that request.
  • The gateway records each request's time, route, price and payment outcome to measure demand, and shows the most recent ones on its public stats page. For a paid call this includes the transaction hash and the paying wallet address, which are already public on the Base blockchain.
  • Do not send personal data you do not need to send. A name sent to the sanctions screen is used only to run that screen.
  • The gateway is hosted on Railway, which may keep standard network logs, including the request address and IP address.
