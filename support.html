Support

One address reaches a person for every Donny Automation product: questions, problems, refunds and lost links.

Email donny.nguyen.business@gmail.com

We usually answer within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, US Central time).

What to put in your email

  • The product name.
  • The email address you used at checkout, or your Shopify store's myshopify address for Barcove.
  • Your Stripe receipt number, if you bought something through Stripe.
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.

Never send a card number or a password. We will never ask for one.

Lost a download link or licence key?

StatementLocal Pro and Self-Publishing Calculator Pro

Your licence key is the id of your Stripe checkout. Reply to your Stripe receipt, or email us from the address you paid with, and we will send the key again.

Audit Prospect List and New Adviser Monitor

The list is delivered as a download link right after payment. Reply to your Stripe receipt, or email us from the address you paid with, and we will send a working link.

Barcove

Nothing to download: the app runs from your Shopify admin. If it is missing, reinstall it from its Shopify App Store listing.
