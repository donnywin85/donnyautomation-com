Barcove · an app for Shopify stores

Let shoppers find a product by its SKU or barcode.

Shoppers type a SKU, a barcode, or the start of a word in a product name, and the search box finds it. Barcove searches SKUs, barcodes, descriptions, tags, vendor and product type, not just titles.

View Barcove on the Shopify App Store Install, plans and billing are handled on the App Store listing.

What it does

  • Match by SKU, barcode, or the start of a word, so a partial SKU still finds the product.
  • Search titles, descriptions, tags, vendor and product type.
  • Synonym groups you control, such as hoodie and sweatshirt, kept until you change them.
  • Build the search index from the app home page with one click. It then updates on its own, up to your plan's product limit.
  • Add the search box to your theme as an app block.
Shopper types: AB-12 finds the product with SKU AB-1234 Shopper types: a barcode finds the product carrying that barcode Shopper types: sweatshirt finds products tagged hoodie (synonym group)

Who it is for

Stores whose customers already know the code of what they want: parts and spares, supplies, hardware, repeat and trade buyers, or anyone reordering from a packing slip or a label. When a shopper has a SKU or a barcode in hand, the default title search often comes back empty. Barcove makes that code searchable.

It also suits stores with many similar products, where the SKU is the quickest way to tell them apart.
