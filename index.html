For Shopify stores whose shoppers buy by part number.

StatementLocallive

For bookkeepers and anyone who needs bank statements in a spreadsheet.

Converts PDF bank and card statements to CSV or Excel inside your browser. The file is not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and each conversion is checked against the balances printed on the statement.
